# Win test 3j.1 (Zoomed graphs), 2026-10-14

Real `yagura.exe` (release), Windows 11 x64, 1200x800 window, UI language Japanese (so `… ago` / `now` read `5 min 前` / `今`). Isolated state/config. Input by the kit's SendInput; pictures by `Save-Shot` (kept under the kit dir `shots\`).

## 3j.1 — ticked `[x]`

- Double click (`Send-DoubleClick`) opened the zoom over the window on every graph tried; each showed the title (with `· 5 分`), the latest value top right, `平均 / 最小 / 最大` and `1 点 1 秒`, a scale at 0/25/50/75/100 %, and `5 min 前` / `2 min 前` / `今` under it. Verified in `z-*.png`, and by pixel (600,400) = 22,24,29 (zoom panel) in all of them:
  - overview: CPU, memory, GPU 1, disk (`z-ov-*`)
  - cpu: total (`cpu-zoom`)
  - mem: memory, swap (`z-mem-*`)
  - gpu: usage, VRAM (`z-gpu-*`)
  - net: receive and send of Proxmox Direct (`z-net-rx`, `z-net-tx`)
  - disk: read, write, busy (`z-disk-r/w/busy`)
  - sensors: GPU temperature (`z-sens`)
- While it was open on cpu: a click at (300,600) and the key `3` changed nothing (`cpu-still.png`: still the cpu tab, status bar still `拡大`).
- `Esc`, `q` and `Enter` each closed it (status bar back to `通常`: `closed-Esc/q/Enter.png`).
